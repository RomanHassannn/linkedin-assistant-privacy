My Assistant Privacy Policy

Draft — pending OAuth setup and final configuration verification.

About this policy

My Assistant is a personal LinkedIn assistant operated by Roman Hassan, currently for the operator’s own LinkedIn account. Contact and data deletion requests: romanhassan6g@gmail.com. This document describes the planned local MCP setup and its inspected storage behavior; OAuth connection has not yet been completed. It must be updated if hosting, providers, users or data handling change.

Information processed

With LinkedIn authorization, the assistant requests openid, profile, email and w_member_social scopes. LinkedIn may provide a member identifier, name, email, profile picture URL, locale and related identity claims. OAuth access tokens, any issued refresh tokens, granted scopes and expiry metadata are processed to maintain the connection. Passwords and one-time codes are entered directly on LinkedIn and are not requested in the assistant chat.

Purposes and current limits

Identity data is used to identify the connected account and help the operator with their own account. Publishing permission is intended for posts explicitly authorized by the operator after setup. The current connection-only adapter exposes login, logout, status, profile and health checks, and blocks post publishing and deletion. The reviewed server does not provide inbox access, full profile editing, job search or job application submission.

Local storage and retention

The configured local adapter uses a .private directory on the operator’s computer for the token file and any future audit/history files. Token records include the LinkedIn member identifier and expiry metadata. Tokens are encrypted by the server; the upstream default key derives from machine attributes, so encryption alone should not be treated as strong protection against another local user. Profile responses are fetched on demand and are not cached by the reviewed server, but they can appear in the MCP client conversation. The server has no scheduled retention cleanup: stored token files remain until logout, manual deletion or replacement, even after token expiry. If publishing tools are enabled later, the upstream implementation records plaintext audit/history entries including timestamps, post identifiers, up to 500 characters of content and, where applicable, URLs or local media paths. Such records remain until manually deleted; logout does not remove them. The optional scheduling pipeline is not enabled.

OpenAI and other processing

The local MCP server is intended for use through ChatGPT/Codex, provided by OpenAI. Tool outputs, profile information, prompts and content supplied to the AI client may therefore be processed by OpenAI and retained in conversations or other service records according to the chosen product, account settings and applicable OpenAI terms and policies. This policy does not claim that all data remains local or promise a specific OpenAI retention period. Credentials and tokens should remain in local configuration and must not be pasted into chat. LinkedIn processes OAuth and API requests under its own policies. No separate hosted application backend, analytics service or advertising service has been selected for this setup. Additional providers require a policy update.

Sharing and public content

Information is used for this personal assistant and is not offered for sale by the operator. Data needed for authorization and enabled API operations is sent to LinkedIn; data supplied to ChatGPT/Codex can be sent to OpenAI. If publishing is enabled and explicitly requested later, post text and selected media will be sent to LinkedIn and shared with the selected LinkedIn audience. The privacy policy itself will be hosted publicly on GitHub Pages; its public operator name and contact email will be visible, and GitHub’s hosting practices apply to visits.

Disconnecting and deleting information

The operator can use logout to delete the local token file, remove the app’s access in LinkedIn account settings, and manually remove local audit/history files or other saved exports. Logout alone does not revoke the app on LinkedIn. Removing local records does not delete posts already on LinkedIn or information already retained by LinkedIn or OpenAI. Conversations and provider-held information must be managed through the respective provider’s controls and request processes. Privacy or deletion questions may be sent to romanhassan6g@gmail.com; this policy does not promise a deletion deadline that the operator has not established.

Changes

This policy must be reviewed before opening the assistant to other users, deploying a backend, enabling additional tools or changing storage/provider arrangements. Last drafted: 23 October 2026.
